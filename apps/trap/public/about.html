We started with four hives and a soldering iron.

Hivewell began in 2021 in a garage in Burlington, Vermont, when two hobby beekeepers lost a colony to a swarm they never saw coming. One of them built a scale out of a bathroom scale and a Raspberry Pi. The other wrote the app.

Today we're 34 people, a warehouse of sensor plates, and a network that watches over 41,000 hives across North America. We still keep bees. Our office has six hives on the roof, and yes, they're on HiveSense.

A share of every sale goes to pollinator habitat restoration through our partners at the Vermont Pollinator Fund.
