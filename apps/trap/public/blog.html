Engineering · Aug 14, 2026

How 41,000 hives phone home

Every HiveSense plate reports every 10 minutes, which adds up to about 2.1 million readings a day. Our telemetry pipeline runs on a small fleet of Node services behind a single gateway, and a Postgres primary keeps the history.

When something goes wrong, the on-call engineer's first stop is our status board. It started as a quick internal Next.js dashboard with a "ping the database from the edge" button for debugging, and it grew from there. You can see the same board from the System status link in our footer.

— Marco, platform team
