HiveSense 2

A 9 mm sensor plate that slides under any Langstroth brood box. Solar-charged, weatherproof, and paired to the Hivewell app over LoRa or your phone's Bluetooth.

What it measures

  • Weight · ±50 g, every 10 minutes
  • Brood temperature & humidity · probe between frames 5 and 6
  • Colony sound · on-device swarm and queenless detection
  • Lid opened · theft and bear alerts

How it works

  1. Slide HiveSense under the brood box. No tools.
  2. Pair it in the Hivewell app and name your hive.
  3. Readings sync to Hivewell Cloud; alerts arrive by push or SMS.
